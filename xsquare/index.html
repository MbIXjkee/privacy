Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including but not limited to:

The collection and processing of this information is performed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies.

Your Choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent form is shown when you first launch the game, asking whether you agree to personalized advertising. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad consent.

On iOS, personalized advertising also requires permission to use the device Advertising Identifier (IDFA). You can grant or withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Withdrawing consent does not remove advertising from the game — ads will continue to be shown, but they will not be personalized.
